# WebMCP Capability Forge Website Design

The official website uses an institutional editorial system: near-black operational surfaces, white and soft-gray explanatory bands, electric blue actions, large display typography, restrained motion, and line-led information architecture. The deterministic lab remains visually connected to the extension while exposing no native WebMCP tools or hidden agent instructions.

The homepage explains and converts. Legal pages prioritize readable long-form text. The lab prioritizes accessible human labels, stable task semantics, and deliberately replaceable selector evidence. Raw colors remain in CSS, gradients are excluded, and reduced-motion behavior is required.
